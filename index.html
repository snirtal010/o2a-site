How we build

Pine is the reference. MT5 has to match it.

Most MT5 versions of TradingView indicators are rewrites that drift. Ours start from the Pine source and are checked against it before release.

Step 1

Write it in Pine

The indicator is designed and tested on TradingView. That source is the truth every other version is measured against.

Step 2

Port it line by line

Pine's own behaviour is copied too: how a higher-timeframe value maps to each chart bar, warm-up periods, and the 500-object drawing limits.

Step 3

Compare bar by bar

The same window is exported from both platforms and compared value by value and signal by signal.

Step 4

Explain every gap

A difference is released only when it is traced to the data feed, never to the logic.

Install

Two minutes on either platform

TradingView

  1. Send us your TradingView username after purchase.
  2. We grant access to the invite-only script.
  3. Open a chart, go to Indicators → Invite-only scripts, and add it.
  4. Settings are grouped by module; defaults match our published screenshots.

MetaTrader 5

  1. Download the .ex5 file and the preset files.
  2. In MT5: File → Open Data Folder, then copy the indicator into MQL5\Indicators\o2a\.
  3. Restart MT5 or refresh the Navigator, then drag the indicator onto a chart.
  4. On a black chart, load the included _dark.set preset from the Inputs tab.

FAQ

Questions

Do the TradingView and MT5 versions show the same signals?

They run the same logic. On the same bars they draw the same signals; where your broker's prices differ from TradingView's feed, a signal sitting exactly on a threshold can fire one bar apart. Each indicator's notes list what was compared and how closely it matched.

Do the indicators repaint?

Signals are confirmed on closed candles, and higher-timeframe values come from the last closed higher-timeframe candle. The forming bar can change until it closes, as on any chart.

Which markets and timeframes?

They work on any symbol. They were developed on gold (XAUUSD) and the Nasdaq 100 on 1-minute to 15-minute charts, with higher-timeframe inputs from 15 minutes to 1 hour.

Is this an automated trading system?

No. These are indicators: they draw and alert. Trading decisions and risk stay with you.

Can I use one licence on two computers?

Yes, for your own accounts. MT5 licences are tied to your account numbers; TradingView access is tied to your username.
